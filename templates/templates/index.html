Who We Are

This project is an AI-powered pediatric health care system focused on diagnosing and rehabilitating fractures in children aged 3 to 15. It combines advanced technologies like Image Processing, NLP, Explainable AI, Agentive AI, and Voice Recognition to deliver precise diagnostics and guided recovery.

Our goal is to support both urban and rural medical practitioners with a complete and interactive solution, making pediatric fracture care more accessible, efficient, and understandable for both doctors and families.

  • Trusted, child-focused medical support.
  • Integrated system for diagnosis and recovery.
  • Clear and user-friendly interface.
  • Improved communication between families and doctors.
  • Accessible solutions for both urban and rural care.
  • Focus on comfort, clarity, and confidence in care.
