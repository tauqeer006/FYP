Common Questions About X-Ai Medical Center

How accurate is the AI diagnosis?

Our AI model has been trained on thousands of X-ray images and achieves high accuracy in fracture detection. All AI diagnoses are reviewed and verified by experienced orthopedic specialists and radiologists before reporting to patients. The system is designed as a diagnostic support tool to assist doctors, not to replace professional medical judgment.

What file formats are accepted for X-ray uploads?

We accept the following image formats for X-ray uploads: JPEG, PNG, DICOM, and PDF. Please ensure your image is clear and of reasonable quality for accurate analysis. Maximum file size is 50MB. If you have questions about your specific image format, please contact us at 0304-5450049.

How long does it take to get a diagnosis?

The AI analysis typically takes 2-5 minutes after uploading your X-ray image. Doctor review and verification usually adds another 5-10 minutes. In most cases, you'll have a complete diagnosis within 15-20 minutes. During peak hours, wait times may be slightly longer. Urgent cases can be marked for priority review.

Can I upload X-rays from other hospitals or diagnostic centers?

Yes, absolutely! You can upload X-ray images from any diagnostic center or hospital. Our system analyzes the images regardless of where they were taken. We recommend having original or high-quality copies of your X-rays for the best analysis results.

What is the exercise monitoring feature?

Our exercise monitoring feature uses your webcam to track your body movements and posture during rehabilitation exercises. The AI provides real-time feedback to ensure you're performing exercises correctly. This helps prevent re-injury and accelerates recovery. The system records your progress for doctors to review.

Is my medical data secure and private?

Yes, we take patient privacy very seriously. All your medical data is encrypted and stored securely on protected servers. Only authorized medical professionals can access your information. We comply with healthcare data protection standards and never share your data without explicit consent. For detailed information about our privacy practices, please see our Privacy Policy.

Do you have an age restriction for patients?

There is no age restriction for fracture diagnosis. Our services are available for patients of all ages, from children to elderly individuals. However, for patients under 18, parental/guardian consent is required. Exercise rehabilitation programs may be modified based on age and medical condition.

What should I do if the AI seems to have missed something?

If you have concerns about your diagnosis, please contact us immediately at 0304-5450049 or email tauqeerqureshi112@gmail.com. Your case will be reviewed by senior medical professionals. We can also arrange a consultation with our specialists for second opinion.

How do I use the voice chat feature?

Our voice-enabled chatbot is available 24/7. Simply click on the chat widget on our website and click the microphone icon to start speaking. You can ask questions about your diagnosis, get exercise guidance, or ask general health questions. The AI will respond to you with voice and text for clarity.

What types of fractures can you detect?

Our system is specifically trained to detect shoulder fractures, including: Proximal humerus fractures, Clavicle fractures, Scapular fractures, and other shoulder-related bone injuries. We are continuously expanding our detection capabilities to cover additional bone fractures. Contact us to ask about specific types of fractures.

Can I get a second opinion from a different doctor?

Yes, we encourage getting a second opinion, especially for complex cases. You can submit your case for review by a different specialist from our team. This incurs an additional consultation fee. Please contact us at 0304-5450049 to arrange this.

How do I book a consultation with a doctor?

You can book a consultation through multiple ways: 1) Fill out our contact form on this website, 2) Call us at 0304-5450049, 3) Email us at tauqeerqureshi112@gmail.com, or 4) Use the appointment form on the home page. Our team will get back to you within 30 minutes during business hours.
