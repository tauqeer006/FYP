🏋️‍♂️ Perform Exercise & Follow Tips

Breathe Deeply

Deep breathing improves lung function and relaxes muscles. Practice 5‑10 minutes daily.

Hydrate Well

Drink at least 6–8 glasses of water to aid respiratory health and general energy.

Light Stretching

Stretch arms and shoulders 3x a day to loosen chest muscles for easier breathing.

Avoid Dust

Keep your room dust-free. Use masks when going outdoors to prevent irritation.
